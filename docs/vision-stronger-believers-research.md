## 1. The vision statement — in his own words

**@2:01:26** — [watch](https://youtube.com/watch?v=7ikShQi33n4&t=7286s)

> "The vision of our ministry is raising stronger believers. And we're not just mouthing words
> like motivational speaking. No. That's a vision. That's an instruction God gave us. We're
> raising stronger believers. So, who is a stronger believer? … The first one is who? One who
> knows God. The second one is who? Who knows his identity in Christ. The third one is who? One
> who knows their rights and privileges."

He frames the five marks as the answer to a second question running through the message: *why do
problems persist so long in the lives of Christians?* Ignorance in these five areas is his
answer. Useful framing for the page — the vision isn't abstract, it's diagnostic.
